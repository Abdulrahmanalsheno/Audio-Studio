# 🎧 Audio Studio

**Split, compress, and convert audio files right in your browser. No uploads, no server, no sign-up.**

Audio Studio is a simple local tool. Your files never leave your computer, because everything is processed inside your browser.

---

## ✨ Features

### ✂️ Split Audio
- Cut a track into a set number of equal parts, or into fixed-length pieces (for example, every 30 seconds)
- See the cut points on a live waveform
- Preview the timestamps before you export
- Download all the pieces together as one ZIP file

### 🗜️ Compress Audio
- Make audio files much smaller by exporting to MP3
- Pick a quick preset: **Smallest**, **Small**, **Balanced**, or **High quality**
- Or set your own bitrate, mono/stereo, and sample rate

### 🔄 Convert Format
- Turn audio files into **MP3** or **WAV**
- Optional parallel encoding with Web Workers for faster results

---

## 🔒 Privacy

All processing happens locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded to any server.

---

## 🚀 Getting Started

1. Go to the [Releases page](../../releases) and download **v1.0.0**.
2. Open it in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, or Safari).
3. Drop in your audio file, choose Split, Compress, or Convert, and download the result.
